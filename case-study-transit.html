Walk NYC

iOS · SwiftUI · MapKit · Solo build

Fewest stops isn't always the best trip.

Sometimes a 15-minute walk down a scenic waterfront corridor beats three transfers underground. This app lets you optimize a subway trip for walking minutes, not just speed, with a "fewest stops" mode and a "target walk time" mode.

The UX

A storyboard of the three core screens: fewest-stops mode, target-walk-time mode, and the avoid/preferred station settings.

Status

Currently working on an MVP - specifically finding the cheapest, most reliable routing service from Point A to Point B.

Early decisions worth naming

Decision

The "avoid list" (stations a user won't walk to) is 100% manually declared by the user - no algorithmic safety scoring of any kind.

Why

Avoid lists are a purely personal decision, to be tied to each user's profile to begin with. If there's a lot of usage, an algorithmic safety scoring mechanism based on crowdsourced input would be spec'ed and added to the backlog.

Decision

Scenic corridors are seeded as a small, hand-picked list of station pairs with a "scenic" flag - not derived from mapped routes or external data.

Why

The scenic future is a pure nice-to-have to incentivize walking, and I wanted to ensure it's trustworthy based on actually scenic areas we've vetted.
